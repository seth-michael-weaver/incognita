## Still refused, and why

Each is refused with TALYS's message or the port's reason, never run silently.

- **Physics not ported:** `preeqmode 4` (MSD/MSC: msd.f90 + continuum DWBA); `resonance y` (Cullen's PREPRO,
  29,688 lines); `fymodel 3` (gefsub.f); `pfnsmodel 2` spectra / `outspectra` / `channels y` with fymodel 4-5
  (ffevap lab transform, pfnsout); multiple pre-equilibrium with `twocomponent n`, `gshell y` or `preeqspin 4`
  (multipreeq.f90); `astro y` + `racap y` (stellar direct-capture rate); `urr` with widthmode 2/3 at a
  width-fluctuation energy; `ejectiles <subset>` (parskip does not reach the compound/pre-equilibrium decay);
  `rpevap y`, `statepot y`, `rotational`, `sysreaction`, `ecisstep` != 0, `ompenergyfile` with `endf y`.
- **Combinations not wired:** `jlmomp y` with `preeqmode 3`, `racap y`, `optmodall y` or `alphaomp 3-5`; energy-local
  forms of the OMP adjust keywords inside JLM/folding; `preeqspin 3` on the GPU path; `ldmodelracap 1/2` with
  direct capture; table forms of `tjadjust`; `mass 0` with astro/urr/integral; `ltarget` with `liso`, other
  projectiles than n, or the angular/spectra/recoil/astro/urr/endf/components/racap/production/massdis outputs.
- **TALYS bugs not reproduced on purpose:** `gnorm y` (compares eV with keV and drives E1 to 0);
  `astrogs n` on a fissile target (wrong compound bin in tfission).
- **External files and paths:** `densfile`, `e1file`, `m1file`, `massdir`, `rescuefile`, `strucpath`, `bestpath`.
- **As TALYS refuses:** `production y` with n or photons, `massdis y` on natural targets, `Ltarget` > 40, legacy-only
  models without `legacy y`, every checkvalue range.

## Deliberate INCOGNITA differences (stay as they are)

A gap counts as deliberate when TALYS mode closes it and one INCOGNITA switch accounts for all of it
(`FIDELITY_DEFAULTS.md` section 1). They are calibrated, promoted engine options:

- **Target level-density window** (`npwindow`): target level density near S_n for targets with no measured D0. Fe-56
  (n,p) 14 MeV +12.1 %, (n,a) +11.0 %; Co-59, Zr-90 +5-8 %.
- **Exact fission-hump transmission** (`exactfis y`): exact 1-D transmission per fission hump instead of the WKB
  table. U-238 fission 5 MeV +9.6 %, 14 MeV +4.3 %; U-238 (n,2n) 14 MeV -3.2 %.
- **Coupled-channel exits to non-band levels** (`ccexitnbnf y`): coupled-channel exits to non-band levels of
  non-fissile targets. Au-197 capture 1 MeV -27.3 %, (n,n') 0.1 MeV +128 %.
- **Low-energy elastic** (`INCOGNITA_LOWEPOT`): no compound elastic below the resolved region (thermal elastic/total
  rows).
- The rest of TALYS mode (`fluxclose`, `chancap`, `nnleak`, `alphatemp`, `sfexit`, `kevleak2`, `heavymacs2`,
  `ftglobal`, `tfold`, `ctwcont`, `alphaentr`, `INCOGNITA_GRIDTOP`, `INCOGNITA_SPECCLOSE`, `INCOGNITA_TARMASSFIX`,
  `INCOGNITA_ISOROTPASS`) moved nothing in the checked cases.
- Output families are off unless their keyword is set, so default `Results` stay bit-identical.

## Port bugs: fixed in the default engine

Port bugs are fixed in the default engine. The four fixes in `physics/hf/fidfix.py` are ON by default;
`INCOGNITA_FIDFIX=0` gives the pre-fix port, and a comma list turns on only the named ones:

- `ldcn` (ldmodelCN reached every chained compound nucleus), `besttables` (best/<nuc>/tables not searched first),
  `fitrisomer` (a fitted risomer of the initial CN acted): these change only decks with `ldmodelCN`, best-file tables
  or `*fit y`, by up to 7.6 % ((n,np), Fe-56 best) towards stock.
- `psfsgl` (tabulated photon strength energies in TALYS's single precision): the only one that acts on default
  decks. Measured on the default engine (`engine_c.run`, grid 1 keV-20 MeV, every `Results` array, fixes off vs on;
  two off runs are bitwise equal): U-238 capture at 0.5 MeV 81.324 -> 81.227 mb (**-0.119 %**, towards stock),
  U-238 fission 5 MeV 685.20 -> 685.14 mb (-0.009 %), every other U-238 cell >= 1 mb <= 1.0e-4 relative (smaller
  cells <= 2.3e-4); Fe-56, Co-59 and Au-197: 254-259 of 650-690 arrays move, all by <= 3.3e-7 relative, none of
  their cells >= 1 mb by more than 1e-6. Details: `FIDELITY_DEFAULTS.md` section 7.
