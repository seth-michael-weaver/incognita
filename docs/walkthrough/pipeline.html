Where it stands

As of 27 October 2026. Losses first.

  • Caught. INCOGNITA's earlier Pu-239 resonance file was a statistical ladder whose fission is about 2× too weak at 20–1000 eV compared with every EXFOR fission set. It puts the PCI-001 plutonium benchmark about 22 % low. It is not released. Five refits to raw EXFOR data also failed their pre-registered gates; the diagnosis points at our model of the experiments (detector normalisations, a thick-sample transmission defect, one capture set modelled as the wrong quantity), not at the measurements.
  • Withdrawn. Zr-94 and Pu-240 were released and withdrawn the same day after failing our EXFOR-only judge: Zr-94's thermal capture is 1.10× the EXFOR value (0.0475 ± 0.0018 b), outside the ±9.7 % limit; Pu-240's thermal fission is 1.22× the n_TOF 2015 measurement (0.0680 ± 0.0052 b), where ENDF/B-VIII.1 is 1.10×.
  • Not released. The own U-238 and O-16 files make benchmark cases worse (U-238 PMF-006 by 90 pcm, beyond 2σ; O-16 four cases by 141–172 pcm).
  • Released. res-v0.1: 7 nuclides (Fe-56, Ni-58, Zr-90, Cu-65, Gd-155, F-19, Na-23) whose resolved resonance region is INCOGNITA's own. Everything else in each file is ENDF/B-VIII.1, so these are hybrids, not full own evaluations.
  • Judged. Na-23 passes the EXFOR-only judge. The other 6 are not cleared: EXFOR has no usable reference yet for at least one of their quantities. That is not a pass.
  • Next. Before any new fit, the fitting chain is being made to reproduce ENDF/B-VIII.1 on the raw data. For Pu-239 the registered test is ENDF/B-VIII.1's own resonance parameters, run through the corrected experiment models, reaching χ²/point ≤ 2 on at least two thirds of the data sets.

Sources: library/res-v0.1/README.md (release, judge verdicts, benchmark changes) and the project's research reports RESINTEG (integration and development benchmarks), PU239DIAG (the Pu-239 diagnosis), JUDGEFIX (the EXFOR-only judge) and DOPPLER (temperature checks), all of 27 October 2026. The drawings marked “illustration” are simulated to show an effect; every other number on this page is from those sources.
